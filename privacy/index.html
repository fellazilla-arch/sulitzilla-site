Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Sulitzilla is operated by SZ GADGETS AND ACCESSORIES SHOP, Tarlac City, Tarlac, Philippines ("Sulitzilla", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you buy from us or message us, how we use it, and how to have it deleted. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

  • Messages and comments. When you message our Facebook Page or Instagram account, comment on our posts, or chat with our Shopee or Lazada shops, we receive your messages, the photos or files you send, and your name or username as shown by that platform.
  • Order details. When you order, we keep what you bought, the price, your name, phone number and delivery address, and the payment method, so we can ship your order and handle warranty or returns.
  • Website. sulitzilla.com shows our prices and stock. It does not ask you to log in and does not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and reply to your messages and comments. Every reply is written by a person on our team; we do not send automated marketing messages.
  • To process, ship and support your orders (warranty, returns, installment payments).
  • To keep our own sales and inventory records.

Our team reads and answers Facebook, Instagram, Shopee and Lazada messages in one inbox that we run ourselves. It reads your messages through each platform's official tools (for Facebook and Instagram, Meta's Messenger and Instagram APIs) and only for the purposes above.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We only share what is needed to complete your order:

  • Couriers (for example LBC) receive your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Payment and installment providers receive what they need to process your payment.
  • Shopee, Lazada, Facebook and Instagram already hold the conversations you have with us on their platforms, under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for warranty, returns and the records the law requires us to keep. Copies of messages in our inbox are kept only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete them at any time (see below).

How to delete your data

To have the information we hold about you deleted, email sulitzilla@gmail.com or message us on Facebook Messenger (m.me/sulitzilla) saying "Delete my data", with the name or username you used to contact us.

We will delete your messages and personal details from our own systems within 30 days and confirm when it's done. Records the law requires us to keep (for example sales receipts) are kept only for the required period and used for nothing else.

Conversations stored by Facebook, Instagram, Shopee or Lazada themselves can be deleted from your account on that platform.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask what information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Contact us using the details below.
